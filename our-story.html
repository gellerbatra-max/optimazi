·Our story

Human intuition, at machine speed.

The marker maker's judgement is decades of craft. The AI's job isn't to replace it — it's to give that judgement more shots on goal, in minutes instead of hours. That belief is why Gelian built Optimazi.

01The problem we started with

The step that decides the margin was the one no one had time for.

Marker making and nesting is where fabric — half the cost of a garment — is won or lost. Yet it's usually done by hand, against a deadline, with no way to compare a second option. We set out to make the best layout the default, on every style, without adding an hour to the schedule.

02How we build

Intuition first

The tools amplify the marker maker's judgement — they never pretend to know the floor better than the people on it.

Browser-native

No installs, no version drift, one dataset from first pattern to final cut. Studio and factory stay in lockstep.

Measured, not hyped

Every efficiency figure is grounded in what the software actually produced — shown before you commit, not in a report days later.

03The company

Gelian (Pvt) Ltd.

Optimazi is developed by Gelian (Pvt) Ltd, based in Rathmalana, Sri Lanka — at the heart of one of the world's great apparel-manufacturing regions, building for fashion houses and the production partners who make for them.
