·Our Story

Twenty thousand hours in the making.

Gelian is a setup built to invent software for the bottlenecks of garment manufacturing — delivering fashion to market with fewer resources, at lower cost, and faster than the competition. Optimazi is our answer to the one that decides a collection's margin.

01The bottleneck

It's a jigsaw puzzle worth millions.

We specialize in marker making — arranging every pattern piece for a garment, or a batch across sizes, onto a layout called the "marker" that's placed on the fabric before cutting.

Think of it like a jigsaw puzzle: fitting every piece — sleeves, collars, fronts, backs, pockets — usually across multiple sizes and garments, onto a single length of fabric with as little wasted space as possible. The tighter the fit, the less fabric is wasted, and that flows straight through the business: utilization to consumption, consumption to fabric cost, fabric cost to the cost of the garment itself.

02The founder

A decade inside the cutting room.

Our founder is a specialist in this space. He spent over a decade as a fabric-consumption planner inside one of Asia's largest apparel production houses — building markers, planning material, and chasing efficiency for some of the biggest fashion brands in the world.

He used every so-called industry-leading marker-making and nesting system available — and still found too much manual work, long waits to reach a workable layout, functional limitations in real use cases, and fabric utilization plateaued at 70–80%. Once you account for those limitations and the manual intervention needed to finish the job, the fabric-cost saving those systems delivered in practice was close to negligible. They lacked the human intuition of how design, marker making and cutting actually behave on a live production floor.

03The secret recipe

Human intuition, then the maths and the AI.

Studying those limitations up close and delivering practical fixes for over a decade led him to architect his own software from the ground up — addressing almost every bottleneck in fabric-utilization optimization that today's so-called industry-leading CAD systems still can't.

What no existing software can offer, and the reason Optimazi reaches 85–90% utilization, is that intuition: over 20,000 hours of hands-on problem-solving in marker making, production planning and fabric-cost management at one of the most advanced garment setups in Asia. We combined it with advanced mathematics and artificial intelligence to forge the most capable fabric-cost software in the market — in both functionality and usability.
